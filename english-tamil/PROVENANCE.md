# Rule scope and maintenance

This rulebook consolidates language and review guidance while keeping application
settings and experimental evidence outside the active instructions. Consult
[the research references](references/sources.md) for linguistic grounding and
annotation limits. Worked examples are authored teaching material.

The loanword map retains its candidate keys and proposed Latin renderings for
compatibility with existing checkers; descriptions specify contextual use. Its category names describe review priority, not calibrated confidence
or context-free replacement permission. Annotation tags, script choices and the
accepted particle inventory are explicit local conventions.

When changing a rule, retain a snapshot and record which new outputs used it.
A new rule does not retroactively validate earlier text. Keep historical snapshots
with experiment evidence; do not load old experimental instructions as additional
rules during ordinary generation. Distribute identical rule copies when the
same guidance is included in multiple packages.
