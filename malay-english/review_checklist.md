# Review checklist

Record `accept`, `repair` or `unresolved` with the actual text, source ID and
concrete findings. Acceptance under a project rubric is not native-speaker
certification. Do not invent reviewer identities or independent approvals.

## Task and meaning

- Identify rewriting, authoring, transcription or annotation mode (R01).
- Compare the entire source/candidate pair. Preserve facts, roles, quantities,
  currencies, time, negation, modality, condition and speech act (R02, R14–R19).
- Preserve unresolved gender, inclusive/exclusive “we,” and referents rather
  than guessing (R13–R15).
- Check quotes, questions and causal/conditional scope (R29–R31).
- Ensure every new detail is allowed by the authoring brief; examples provide
  patterns, not facts to import (R36).

## Grammar and register

- Explain the clause and noun-phrase constructions (R04–R12).
- Check the relevant Malay affix, argument structure and voice; avoid mechanical
  additions of `to`, `-ed`, `-s`, `punya` or a light verb (R20–R28).
- Check formal versus conversational expectations separately. Preserve a real
  speaker's nonstandard form in a verbatim transcript (R03, R37–R38).
- Review particles for intended stance and regional fit, including free `kan`
  versus suffix `-kan` (R33).
- Read the sentence aloud or have a competent speaker do so when validating
  spoken naturalness; written grammatical analysis alone is insufficient.

## Language evidence and annotation

- Identify actual Malay and English spans in context; never infer language from
  Latin script alone (R34).
- Check loans, homographs, names, acronyms, fillers and particle-only mixing
  before classifying a lexical switch (R35–R38).
- Preserve `AMBIG`, `SHARED`, `OTHER` and `UNCLEAR` decisions with reasons.
- Verify offsets against the exact surface text and preserve original references.
- Treat morphological switches separately from whole-word boundaries (R24, R40).

## Speech and evaluation, where applicable

- Check the intended pronunciation profile and shared-word reading (P01–P09).
- Listen for correct realization of both languages, intact words at boundaries,
  reference mismatches and nearby speech introduced by padding (P10–P11).
- Record whether evidence is text-only or audio-confirmed; never infer listening
  from a file checksum or a language tag.
- Preserve held-out status and disclose benchmark exposure. Record each selection
  or exclusion rule and the applicable duration, not an unexplained aggregate.

## Repairs

Name the failed rule, the problematic span and the reason. Propose the smallest
meaning-preserving repair, then recheck the whole utterance. A spelling fix can
change language identity; a grammar fix can change agency. Keep unresolved cases
visible. Do not reject naturally short switches for failing an invented ratio,
or accept awkward text because it contains enough words from both languages.
